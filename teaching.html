Teaching

I have taught a range of courses in chemistry, physics and mathematics at different academic levels, including introductory lectures for first-year students as well as advanced laboratory and exercise courses.

I see teaching as a relational process that thrives on trust, clarity, mutual respect, and a supportive learning environment. I believe that learning is a continuous process and that all students are capable of developing new competencies when supported and encouraged.
